MakerWorld → Flash Studio

Converts Bambu Studio and MakerWorld .3mf projects so they open and slice in Flash Studio for the FlashForge Creator 5 Pro. Conversion runs in your browser; files are never uploaded.

What it changes
  • Lowers the Bambu Studio version stamp so Flash Studio doesn't reject files from newer Bambu Studio releases.
  • Swaps the Bambu printer, process and filament presets for Creator 5 Pro ones, keeping the designer's walls, infill, supports, brim and other print choices.
  • Re-centres every plate for the 256 × 256 mm bed and moves the prime tower clear of the parts.
  • Fixes settings Flash Studio considers out of range, and removes pre-sliced Bambu G-code.
  • Maps filament slots onto the four toolheads.

Prefer the command line? npx bambu2ff convert file.3mf
